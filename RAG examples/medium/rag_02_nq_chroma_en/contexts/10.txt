George Washington was an American military officer, statesman, and Founding Father who served as the first president of the United States from 1789 to 1797. As commander of the Continental Army, Washington led Patriot forces to victory in the American Revolutionary War against the British. He presided over the Constitutional Convention of 1787, which established the U.S. Constitution. Washington has been called the "Father of his Country" for his manifold leadership in the formative days of the nation.
