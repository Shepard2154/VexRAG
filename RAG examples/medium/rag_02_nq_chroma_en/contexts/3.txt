Canberra is the capital city of Australia. Founded following the federation of the colonies of Australia as the seat of government for the new nation, it is Australia's largest inland city and the eighth-largest Australian city overall. The site of Canberra was selected for the location of the nation's capital in 1908 as a compromise between rivals Sydney and Melbourne, Australia's two largest cities. Construction of the city began in 1913, and an international design competition won by Walter Burley Griffin and Marion Mahony Griffin yielded a plan with geometric motifs such as circles, hexagons, and triangles.
