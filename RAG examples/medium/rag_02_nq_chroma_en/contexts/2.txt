Romeo and Juliet is a tragedy written by William Shakespeare early in his career about two young Italian star-crossed lovers whose deaths ultimately reconcile their feuding families. It was among Shakespeare's most popular plays during his lifetime and, along with Hamlet, is one of his most frequently performed plays. Today the title characters are regarded as archetypal young lovers. Scholars are divided on the date of the first performance, but most accept that it was written at some point between 1591 and 1595.
