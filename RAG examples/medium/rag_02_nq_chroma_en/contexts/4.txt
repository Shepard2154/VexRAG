Victory in Europe Day is the day celebrating the formal acceptance by the Allies of World War II of Germany's unconditional surrender of its armed forces on Tuesday, 8 May 1945, marking the end of World War II in Europe. Several countries observe public holidays on the day each year, also called Victory Over Fascism Day, Liberation Day, or Victory Day. In the United Kingdom, the principal celebrations take place on the closest Sunday to 8 May, known as VE Day, with commemorations also on 8 May itself when it falls on a weekday.
